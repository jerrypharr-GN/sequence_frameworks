## What Lob Does (for context)

Lob is a direct mail automation platform combining campaign management software, APIs, and an owned/managed nationwide print-and-delivery network — not just a software layer that outsources printing to third parties. They serve 12,000+ companies, concentrated in financial services, healthcare, insurance, retail/ecommerce, and SaaS, with in-house marketing teams, agencies, operations teams, and service providers as primary users.

Source: [lob.com](https://www.lob.com/)

## Competitors Considered

PostGrid, Postie, Postalytics, Click2Mail — as specified by Jerry. Based on public research, PostGrid is likely Lob's closest direct competitor (also API-first, also claims a ~2-day SLA and enterprise compliance certifications); Postie and Postalytics compete more on marketing-campaign analytics/targeting than infrastructure; Click2Mail competes at the low end (self-serve, no minimums, less technical/enterprise-oriented).
